Version: 2.1.3
Date: 2026-11-07
  Changes:
    - Increased Oil rig collection radius so that it can be placed off-center on the Offshore oil patch, to allow for collection of denser oil fields.
    - Added "Offshore oil" as a visible trigger on "Oil processing" technology.
    - Added "Deep sea oil extraction" as a prerequisite to "Oil processing". NOTE: You only need one, either "Oil gathering" OR "Deep seal oil extraction", to collect oil and unlock "Oil processing".
    - Removed prerequisite of "Tanker ship" from "Deep sea oil extraction". You can always take oil to land by pipeline.
    - Added compatibility with Simple Seablock and other mods that hide pumpjacks.
